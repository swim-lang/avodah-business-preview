Mergers & Acquisitions

At Avodah, our mergers and acquisitions (M&A) team supports business owners through the entire acquisition or sale process. We provide expert legal advice on deal structuring, due diligence, regulatory compliance, and contract negotiations. Whether you’re acquiring a new business or selling your company, we ensure the process is smooth and legally structured to minimize your risk and maximize your value.

We consistently represent buyers and sellers in various equity purchases or asset sales. With a focus on Virginia-based transactions, we leverage our local knowledge and experience to handle both simple and complex deals, ensuring a seamless transition for all parties involved.

02

Venture Capital & Fundraising

Securing the right funding for your business can be complex, but our team simplifies the process with tailored legal advice. We assist startups and growing companies in negotiating term sheets, drafting investor agreements, and managing compliance. Whether you are raising a friends and family round, seed funding, or a Series A round with NVCA documents, we are familiar with all kinds of capital raises and could guide you through the process while protecting your interests at every stage.

A key part of fundraising is understanding your cap table and negotiating good governing documents, both of which we guide entrepreneurs through.

Entity Formation & Partnership Structuring

Starting a new business or restructuring an existing one requires careful planning. At Avodah, we provide comprehensive entity formation services, helping you choose the right structure—whether it’s an LLC or corporation—as well as structure your governing documents. Our team drafts and reviews essential agreements to ensure compliance with state laws and protect your business interests.

We also specialize in partnership structuring, creating agreements that align with your vision and minimize future disputes. From operating agreements to joint ventures, our Virginia business attorneys help you build a solid foundation for long-term success.

Commercial Contracts

Contracts form the backbone of any business, and our team ensures that yours are robust and enforceable. At Avodah, we draft, review, and negotiate all types of commercial agreements, from supplier contracts to service agreements. Our goal is to protect your business interests and ensure that every contract serves your needs.

We pride ourselves on making contracts as brief and simple as possible. However, we also understand the importance of robust contracts that minimize risk and prevent disputes. With our expertise in commercial law, we ensure that every agreement is tailored to your business and legally sound.

05

Outside General Counsel

Our outside general counsel services offer a cost-effective solution for businesses that need ongoing legal support but don’t require a full-time Virginia attorney. We act as your in-house legal team, providing advice on everyday matters such as compliance, employment law, and contract negotiations, including managing hiring outside experts for in-depth regulatory matters. For many clients, this reduces their costs drastically.

By working with Avodah, you gain access to experienced legal counsel without the overhead of hiring an in-house lawyer. We develop long-term relationships with our clients, ensuring we understand your business and can provide proactive legal solutions as you grow.
