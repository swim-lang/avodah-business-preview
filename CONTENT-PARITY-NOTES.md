# Content parity notes

## Purpose

This rebuild is a brand and technical refresh of Earley Business Legal. The existing public site remains the content authority until Avodah approves a deliberate content change. Preserving useful rankings and search intent takes priority over rewriting copy for style.

## Preserved in this pass

- Existing practice focus: business formation, M&A, commercial contracts, outside general counsel, and fixed-fee Handshake documents.
- Known WordPress page paths and information architecture.
- Phone number and the currently represented Richmond and Leesburg offices.
- Existing page titles, descriptions, headings, body copy, testimonials, service boundaries, guide content, and fixed-fee product descriptions already present in the rebuild.
- Parked blog and Lorem templates remain unpublished, outside the sitemap, and outside the deployment artifact.
- The Operating / Partnership Agreement price is confirmed at $3,500.
- Sitewide Privacy and Terms links are intentionally omitted. The Handshake Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions offerings remain available as fixed-fee legal products.

## Small non-substantive changes made

- Replaced two placeholder family-site links with the confirmed Avodah Employment and Avodah Traffic destinations.
- Added machine-readable metadata, structured data, image attributes, clean internal URLs, crawl controls, redirects, and analytics hooks.
- Removed the desktop/mobile review control from ordinary site pages. The standalone viewer remains available for internal QA.

## Intentionally not changed

- No service-page or guide copy was rewritten.
- No new geographic claims, rankings, awards, review totals, results, response promises, or attorney credentials were added.
- No archived blog content was published.
- No production form, domain, Search Console property, analytics stream, DNS record, or live-site setting was changed.

## Items still needing confirmation

- Final domain and canonical host.
- A crawl/export of the current WordPress site to catch any legacy URLs not listed in the repository README.
- Current office, phone, roster, and credentials accuracy.
- Contact-form routing and retention.
- Attorney review and publication approval.
