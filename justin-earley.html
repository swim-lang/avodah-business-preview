Justin Earley · Richmond, Virginia
Profile

Justin is a business lawyer who helps companies with a wide variety of legal issues. If your company is forming, seeking funding or investment, hiring a key employee, negotiating a major agreement, considering a franchise, or approaching a sale, Justin can help.

Justin graduated from the University of Virginia with a degree in English Literature before spending four years in Shanghai, China, teaching and writing. He got his law degree from the Georgetown University Law Center and began his legal career in big law in the mergers and acquisitions practice of McGuireWoods LLP.

In addition to his business law practice, Justin frequently speaks on the state of mental health in the legal profession and the impact work and technology habits have on mental health. In 2019, Justin was appointed by the Supreme Court of Virginia to the board of the Virginia Judges and Lawyers Assistance Program. If you are interested in having Justin speak at your legal event, you can read more and get in touch at justinwhitmelearley.com.

He is married to Lauren and has four sons – Whit, Asher, Colt and Shep. In his spare time, Justin enjoys writing, spending evenings with friends, exploring Richmond’s restaurants with his wife Lauren, and exploring the James River hiking trails with his four boys.

Author

He is also the author of the award-winning book on formational habits, The Common Rule – Habits of Purpose for an Age of Distraction, and a book on parenting, Habits of the Household.
