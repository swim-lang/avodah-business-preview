How this practice began

“Most legal questions aren’t as complicated as people think.”

Hi. I’m Justin, and I founded this practice five years ago with a vision to make business law understandable. After working for years in Big Law on Fortune 500 deals, I realized that most legal questions aren’t as complicated as people think. Contracts can be written in simple language and business law doesn’t have to cost a fortune.

We love what we do because we solve people’s problems every day. But you’ll love it because we solve them affordably. So whether you need sophisticated counsel for ongoing contract review, or whether you just need to set up an LLC, we’ll solve it for you.
