What do commercial contracts cover?

Commercial contracts are the playbook for your business relationships. They outline who is responsible for what, when, and how things need to happen, and how to handle any bumps in the road. Here are just a few common types of contracts businesses rely on daily:

Lease Agreements

Define the terms for renting office or retail space

Sales Agreements

Outline the terms for selling products or services

Vendor Agreements

Establish the terms for buying goods or services

Employment Contracts

Set expectations for roles and responsibilities, pay, and policies

Partnership Agreements

Outline the rules for working with business partners

Without solid contracts, things can get messy fast. Misunderstandings lead to disputes, which interrupt business. Poorly written agreements may not hold up in court when you need to enforce them. A Virginia lawyer could help you avoid these challenges by drafting comprehensive commercial contracts that protect your business and interests.
