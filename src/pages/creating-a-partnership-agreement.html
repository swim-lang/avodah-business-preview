What to include in your partnership agreement

One of the most important terms of the partnership agreement is to clearly identify who owns the entity and their respective ownership percentages. Furthermore, the document should address how these owners will handle tax issues and share the company’s profits.

A well-drafted partnership agreement will also outline the working relationship between the company’s owners and management. In addition to the terms previously mentioned, the document should also address issues including:

  • Dispute resolution processes
  • Processes for liquidating the company
  • Restrictions on owners transferring their interests
  • Processes for additional capital calls or contributions
  • Processes for exploring a potential sale of the company
  • Policies for owners accessing company books and records
  • Policies for protecting intellectual property and confidential information
  • Estate planning procedures to buy out a partner in cases of death or divorce
