02

Common legal pitfalls to avoid in your business sale

Many legacy owners are surprised to learn just how much is negotiable—and how much is at stake. Without the right legal guidance, you might unintentionally take on future liabilities, leave money on the table, or agree to unfavorable terms in an earn-out or seller financing arrangement. At Avodah, we’ll help you:

  • Decide whether to structure the deal as an asset sale or a stock sale (and understand the tax implications of each)
  • Avoid “survival period” traps or unnecessarily broad exposure in indemnification clauses
  • Clarify how working capital adjustments might impact your final purchase price
  • Understand what to expect with broker fees
  • Avoid earn-out expectations that aren’t feasible and clarify your noncompete obligations
  • Minimize escrow or holdback amounts required by the buyer
  • Prepare a Letter of Intent (LOI) that sets the right tone for the rest of the deal
  • Make sure your key employees are protected
  • Make sure you have a good lease if you are holding onto any real estate
  • Negotiate the deal in a manner that reflects your values and expectations
  • Ensure you understand ALL the documents you’re signing

The most important part of this process is getting us involved before the Letter of Intent stage.

If you’ve already signed an LOI, don’t worry—but reach out as soon as possible. The earlier we’re involved, the more we can shape the deal structure in your favor and help you avoid common pitfalls before you’re locked in.
