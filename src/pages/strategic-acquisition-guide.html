Why strategic buyers face unique legal risks

Strategic acquisitions can unlock tremendous value—but only if structured correctly. You may be inheriting employees, contracts, leases, IP, or litigation risk. And if you already run a business, your attention is divided, which makes it even more essential to have a legal team that can spot landmines before they become liabilities.

02

What to watch for in a strategic acquisition

In any strategic acquisition, structuring the deal properly is everything. A poorly structured deal can saddle you with unknown liabilities, lock you into unfavorable lease terms, or expose you to post-closing financial risk. That’s why your legal team must go beyond reviewing documents—they need to shape the deal to protect your upside. At Avodah, we help you:

  • Identify and limit exposure to hidden or contingent liabilities
  • Structure the deal (asset vs. equity) to avoid successor liability when possible
  • Review and renegotiate lease terms to ensure they’re workable long-term
  • Secure buyer-friendly indemnification provisions that hold up
  • Push for clean reps and warranties that don’t shift risk unfairly to you

The goal is to walk away with what you need to grow—without inheriting what you don’t.
