About Andrew

Prior to joining the firm, Andrew began his legal career practicing in big law at Troutman Pepper Hamilton Sanders LLP, litigating consumer financial services matters across the country. Additionally, he served as a judicial law clerk to the Honorable Henry E. Hudson in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia.

Before entering the legal field, Andrew worked for five years at CapTech, a national technology consulting firm, working with numerous Fortune 500 clients. He also spent a year serving with Manna Project International, an NGO in Managua, Nicaragua, where he led a variety of community development initiatives.

Andrew obtained his law degree from the University of Richmond School of Law, where he graduated magna cum laude and served on the executive board of the University of Richmond Law Review. Andrew holds a bachelor’s degree in business management from Virginia Tech’s R.B. Pamplin College of Business.

Outside the office

Andrew and his wife, Fiona, live in Bon Air with their daughter.
